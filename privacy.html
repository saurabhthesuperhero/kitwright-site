Kitwright: Drum Tuner & Lugs (Android, package com.saikaizen.drumtuner), by Saikaizen Innovations. Last updated 25 November 2026.

Microphone

Kitwright uses the microphone only while a tuning screen is open, to measure the pitch of your drum. The sound is analysed on your phone in real time. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn the permission off at any time in Android settings.

Data stored on your phone

Your kits, drum settings, tuning readings, history, badges and preferences are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Services from Google and partners

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): anonymous usage events (for example "steady reading", "lug pass complete", screen views), app version, device model, OS version and approximate country, used to improve the app. No audio and no personal names are sent.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): crash reports (stack trace, device model, OS version) so we can fix crashes.
  • Google AdMob with the Google User Messaging Platform: free users may see a banner on the kit, history and settings screens and an occasional full-screen ad after saving a kit. Ads are never shown on the tuning screens. AdMob may use the advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads, and personalised ads only with your consent where the law requires it. Ad partners through AdMob mediation: Meta Audience Network and Liftoff (Vungle). You can change your choices any time in Settings, "Privacy options".
  • Google Play Billing: the one-time Pro purchase is handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • Google Play in-app review and in-app updates: may show a rating dialog or update offer provided by Google Play.

Links: Google privacy policy, Firebase privacy, Meta privacy policy, Liftoff privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn them on, Kitwright schedules local reminders on your phone (for example "re-check your kit"). No server is involved.

Children

Kitwright is meant for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Your choices and contact

You can reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings, change ad consent in the app's Settings, and remove all app data by uninstalling. Questions or deletion requests about analytics data: superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com.
