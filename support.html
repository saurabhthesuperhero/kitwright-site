Getting steady readings

  • Put the phone 30–60 cm from the drum, microphone side towards it.
  • Mute other drums (a towel on them). A ringing neighbour drum is the most common cause of a wrong number.
  • Centre pitch: hit the centre with the same stick height and let it ring. STEADY lights when three hits agree.
  • Lugs: tap lightly about 3 cm in from each tension rod. Kitwright reads the overtone there on purpose.
  • "Too loud": move the phone back. "A bit harder": hit a little harder or raise the sensitivity in Settings.

Pro and purchases

Delete data

All kits and readings live only on your phone; uninstalling deletes them. For analytics data, email us.
